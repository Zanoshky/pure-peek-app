How Pure Peek works

Open a file

Pure Peek has no file browser of its own and needs no storage permission. Files reach it the ways Android offers:

Open with
Tap a file in Files, Gmail, Drive or Downloads. When Android asks which app to use, pick Pure Peek, then Always, so that kind of file opens straight away next time.
Share
Share any file, or several, to Pure Peek from another app.
Open files
On home, opens Android's file picker. Pick one file or many.
Open a folder
Pick a folder once and see its files as a grid or a list, newest first or by name. Open one and step through the rest with Previous file and Next file.
Photos
Opens Android's photo picker.
Recent files
Home lists what you opened lately. Turn this off in Settings, Privacy.

If another app always opens a kind of file, Settings, Opening files names it and takes you to its info page, where Open by default, Clear defaults hands the choice back.

The viewer bar

Every file opens with its name, kind and size at the top. The buttons there: Back, Find in this file (where the file has text), About this file, and More. More holds Share, Open with another app and the actions for that kind of file.

About this file shows the size in bytes, the kind, everything the file says about itself, and a SHA-256 checksum on request.

Photos

Photos open at full resolution, drawn in tiles, so zooming into a big photo stays sharp. Pinch to zoom, double tap for actual pixels, double tap again to fit. JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, AVIF, GIF, DNG, BMP and more. Ultra HDR and wide colour photos show in full on screens that can.

About this file lists pixels, megapixels, colour space, camera, lens, exposure, ISO and when it was taken. More has Turn a quarter and Use as wallpaper or contact photo. With Pro, Share a clean copy sends a copy with the place and camera details taken out and leaves the original alone.

PDF

Pages render again at every zoom, so small print stays crisp. More has Go to page, First page, Last page, Copy the text of this page, Print, and on dark looks, Night pages, which shows PDFs as light text on dark. A PDF opens on the page you left it. A locked PDF asks for its password. On a tablet or a wide screen, a page strip sits beside the document.

Sign a PDF or a photo

Tap the pen in the bar, or More, Sign this PDF or Sign this photo. Choose a signature or draw a new one with your finger, in black or blue ink. On the next screen, drag the signature into place, drag its corner or pinch to change its size, or use Smaller and Bigger. With TalkBack, the signature has Move left, right, up and down actions. Turn pages with the arrows to sign another page. Sign makes a new signed copy: save it to your files, share it, or open it. The file you opened is never changed.

A PDF keeps everything it had: the signature is added on top as a new part of the file, so any digital signature already in it keeps covering what it covered. A photo is signed at full size and saved again as a JPEG, or as a PNG when it has see through parts, without its location and camera details.

Your signatures stay on this phone, inside Pure Peek. They are not backed up and do not move to a new phone. See and delete them in Settings, Signatures.

Share a clean copy (Pro)

Files say more than they show. A photo can hold where it was taken and which phone took it; a Word or Excel file names its author, who last saved it and the company; comments carry names; songs and videos carry tags, places and dates; and a camera file name like PXL_20261030_123456.jpg tells the second it was taken. More, Share a clean copy makes a copy with all of that taken out and hands it to the app you pick. What the file shows stays exactly the same: no new compression, the same pages, the same sound.

  • Photos: JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC and AVIF lose GPS, camera, lens, dates, owner and serial fields, XMP, IPTC, comments and content credentials. A JPEG keeps its colour profile and which way up it goes, and an Ultra HDR photo keeps its HDR. A Motion Photo's video is cut off.
  • PDF: the copy is written fresh, with no author, creator, producer, dates, XMP or file ID, and no names on comments, and photos placed in it lose their camera details. A digital signature cannot stay valid in a changed file, so the copy leaves it out and says so.
  • Word, Excel and PowerPoint (and the LibreOffice formats): author, last saved by, company, manager and dates go, comment and change authors lose their names, and pictures inside are cleaned like photos.
  • Songs and videos: MP3 and FLAC lose their tags and cover art; MP4, MOV, M4A and 3GP lose their place, tags and dates.
  • Zip: every photo, PDF, Office file, song and video inside is cleaned the same way, a zip inside it too, and their names lose their dates. Every file in the copy gets the same date, and the zip loses its comment. Other files go across unchanged; a file that cannot be cleaned is left out, and Pure Peek names it.
  • The name: dates and times come out of it, and a camera name becomes photo, video or audio.

Pro is optional: the app you bought is complete without it. It is one purchase through Google Play, with no subscription, and it unlocks every Pro extra that comes later. Get it from the Pro sheet that opens when you choose Share a clean copy, or from Settings, Pro, which also has Restore purchase for a new phone. Pure Peek still has no internet permission, and the copy is made on the phone.

Tables

CSV, TSV and Excel files open as tables with the header row and the row numbers pinned while you scroll. Tap a column name for Sort smallest to largest, Sort largest to smallest, Column stats (sum, average, smallest, largest, most common values) and Copy the column. Excel files with several sheets show a tab for each sheet. More has Copy as CSV for the whole table, and a switch for files whose first row is data, not names; Settings sets the default.

Text, code and documents

Text, logs and code open with line numbers and wrap; both can be turned off in Settings. JSON has As saved and Tidy tabs, Markdown has Formatted and Source, and Word documents open as clean text. Bigger text and Smaller text are in More. Big files open fast: only the lines on screen are drawn, and a 52 MB log searches in a moment.

A file with the wrong name, like a picture saved as .bin, opens as what it really is, with a note. Anything Pure Peek cannot show opens as bytes.

Find

Find in this file works in text, code, tables and PDFs. Matches are marked, the current one stands out, and Previous match and Next match step through them. The count stops at 20,000 and shows 20,000+.

Web pages and SVG

HTML files and SVG drawings show inside the app with scripts off. Nothing outside the file is loaded, so a page cannot call home. Copy the source is in More.

Archives, audio, video and fonts

Zip and tar
Walk the folders inside a zip, tar, tar.gz or tgz and tap any file to open it, without unpacking the rest. A single .gz file, like a packed log, opens as the file inside. A tar has no table of contents, so a big tar.gz takes a moment to list.
Audio and video
Play and pause, a seek bar, Back 10 seconds and On 10 seconds, speed, and Repeat. Audio shows its cover art and tags.
Fonts
A sample at every size and a box to try your own words.

What Pro adds

Optional extras with Pro. Everything is made on this phone, and the files you pick are never changed. Opening and viewing every file never needs it.

  • Edit, merge and split PDFs: turn, reorder and take out pages, or save pages as pictures
  • Black out parts of a PDF or photo, with what is under the boxes truly gone
  • Sign PDFs and photos
  • Photos to one PDF
  • Compare two files line by line
  • Clean copies with the place, camera, author, dates and other details about you taken out, one file or many at once
  • Sort a table and see column totals and averages
  • Unpack files from 7z and RAR archives and from zips locked with a password
  • Make a PDF smaller to a size you pick, and shrink photos for email
  • Lock a copy of a PDF with a password, or make a copy without one
  • Find and black out words, phone numbers, IBANs and email addresses
  • Convert and resize photos: HEIC, AVIF, WebP, PNG and JPG to JPG, PNG or WebP
  • Choose what a clean copy of a photo or video keeps, like the date without the place
  • Watermarks on PDFs and photos, and page numbers on PDFs
  • Read text, Markdown, Word and PDF text aloud, also with the screen off
  • Read the text in photos and scans, on the phone with no internet

One payment, yours for good. No subscription, no account. A control that needs Pro says (Pro) after its name and opens the Pro sheet.
