Cookbook

Short recipes

A few taps each. Recipes marked Pro need Pro; the rest are free.

Open every PDF in Pure Peek

  1. Tap a PDF in Files, Gmail or Downloads.
  2. When Android asks, pick Pure Peek, then Always.
  3. Another app took it? Settings, Opening files names it; open its info page and Clear defaults.

Read a book

  1. Open an EPUB or FB2 file with Pure Peek.
  2. Swipe or tap the sides to turn pages. Contents jumps to a chapter.
  3. Close it any time. It opens again on the same page.

Read a comic

  1. Open Pure Peek, Open a folder, and pick the folder with your CBZ or CBR files.
  2. Open the first one and swipe. After the last page, the next comic starts.

Look inside a RAR or a 7z

  1. Open the archive with Pure Peek.
  2. Walk its folders and tap a file to see it.
  3. Locked? Tap Type the password; even a locked 7z opens for free.

Send a photo without the place (Pro)

  1. Open the photo, then More, Share a privacy copy.
  2. Pick the app to send it with. Your photo is left as it was.

Sign a PDF (Pro)

  1. Open the PDF and tap the pen, or More, Sign this PDF.
  2. Pick or draw a signature, drag it into place, then Sign.
  3. Save, share or open the signed copy.

Black out a phone number (Pro)

  1. Open the PDF or photo, then More, Find and black out.
  2. Pick phone numbers, or type the words to find.
  3. Check the boxes and save the copy. What was under them is gone.

Get the text out of a scan (Pro)

  1. Open the photo or scanned PDF.
  2. More, Read the text in this photo, or Read the text in this page (for scans).
  3. Copy it. It is read on the phone, with no internet.

Listen to a document (Pro)

  1. Open a text, Markdown, Word file, book or PDF.
  2. More, Read aloud. It keeps reading with the screen off.

Support Pure Peek monthly

  1. Settings, Support the developer.
  2. Tap Support And Live API. Google Play shows the price and how to cancel.

Optional, and it unlocks nothing. Tap the row again to manage or cancel it.
