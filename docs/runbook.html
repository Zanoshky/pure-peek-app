When something goes wrong

What you see, why it happens, and what fixes it. More answers are in the questions.

Pure Peek is not in Open with

Another app is set to always open that kind of file, so Android does not ask.

  1. Settings, Opening files in Pure Peek names the app.
  2. Open its info page, Open by default, Clear defaults.
  3. Tap the file again and pick Pure Peek, Always.

A file opens as bytes

Pure Peek does not know the format, or the file is damaged. It still shows the size and the checksum.

  1. Check the name ending: a file with the wrong ending opens as what it really is, with a note.
  2. Report it with the kind of file, and a sample if it holds nothing private.

A book will not open

Books with DRM, and MOBI and AZW3 files, cannot be read. An EPUB without its list of contents cannot either.

  1. Get a DRM free EPUB from the shop, or convert the book on a computer.

A big archive is slow to open

A 7z, a RAR or a locked zip has to be read from a real file. One from shared storage is copied inside Pure Peek first.

  1. Wait for the copy; it happens once each time you open it.

A RAR or comic says it is locked

Some RAR files hide even their file names behind a password, and a locked comic does not ask for one.

  1. Unpack it on a computer, or ask the sender for a version without a password.

A photo shows no place

Android hides the place from apps without the media location permission, and Pure Peek does not ask for it. About this file says when this happened.

Read aloud stops

Read aloud uses the phone's voice engine. A PDF needs Android 15 or newer for its page text.

  1. Android settings, Text to speech: check a voice is installed and try Play there.

Pro is missing on a new phone

  1. Sign in to Google Play with the account that bought it.
  2. Open Pro in the bottom bar and tap Restore purchase.

The subscription will not start

Support And Live API runs through Google Play. With no account on the phone, an old Play Store, or a copy not from Google Play, it cannot start.

  1. Open the Play Store once and check you are signed in.
  2. Update Pure Peek and the Play Store, then try the row again.

To cancel: tap the same row, or Play Store, your profile, Payments and subscriptions.
